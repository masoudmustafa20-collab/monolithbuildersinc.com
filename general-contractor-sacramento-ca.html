Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for home remodeling in Sacramento?

Permit requirements depend on the type of work and property location. Structural modifications, additions, and certain electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work may require permits. Confirm requirements with the appropriate city or county building department.

Can I build an ADU in Sacramento?

ADUs are permitted under applicable California laws and local development standards, subject to property-specific conditions and design requirements. Reviewing the site and proposed project is an important early step.

How much does a Sacramento home remodel cost?

Remodeling costs depend on project size, material selections, existing conditions, design changes, and construction complexity. Contact us to discuss your plans and request an estimate.

What information should I provide for an estimate?

A description of the work, project photos, approximate measurements, preferred materials, budget range, and desired schedule can help communicate your construction goals.
