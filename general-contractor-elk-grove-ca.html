Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for remodeling in Elk Grove?

Permit requirements depend on the project. Structural changes, additions, and certain electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work may require permits. Check with the appropriate building department before starting construction.

Can I build an ADU on my Elk Grove property?

ADU eligibility depends on applicable California law, local development standards, and the conditions of the property. Reviewing the proposed design and site requirements is an important first step.

How much does kitchen or bathroom remodeling cost?

Remodeling costs depend on room size, selected materials, existing conditions, and construction complexity. Contact Monolith Builders Inc. to discuss your project and request an estimate.

What should I prepare before requesting an estimate?

It helps to have a description of the work, photos of the existing space, approximate measurements, your preferred materials, and an estimated budget and timeline.
