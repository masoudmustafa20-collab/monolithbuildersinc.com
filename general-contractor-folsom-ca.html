Permit requirements depend on the scope of work. Structural modifications, home additions, and certain electrical, plumbing, and mechanical improvements may require permits. Confirm requirements with the City of Folsom.

Can I build an ADU on my Folsom property?

ADU projects are subject to applicable California law, local standards, and property-specific conditions. A review of the property and proposed design is important before proceeding.

How much does a home renovation cost?
